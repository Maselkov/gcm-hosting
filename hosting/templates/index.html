GTNH Craft Monitor

Web AE2 for your GTNH base. See what your crafting CPUs are making and which steps are stuck, what's left in the ME network and how full your LSC is, from any browser.

{% if discord %}Sign in with Discord{% endif %} Or self-host it →

{% if dev_login %}
{% endif %}
  • Crafts: every CPU's job, a live tree of its steps, and a notification when a pinned one finishes.
  • Network: every item, fluid and essentia, with stock history to see what's running out.
  • Power: your LSC's stored EU over time, and how long until it's full or empty.
  • Request crafts from the page, or have items kept in stock for you.

To set it up, sign in, make a base (it gets an address like copper-falcon-4821.{{ domain }}) and paste one command into an OpenComputers computer in game. It needs an Internet Card, which some multiplayer servers turn off.
